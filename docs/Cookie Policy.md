### 1. What Cookies Are and Why We Use Them

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website.

We use cookies to ensure you get the best experience on our Website, to make our Website work, as well as to provide us with useful information about your journey on our Website.

We also use cookies to enable important security features of our Application.

Cookies also help us to analyze traffic and usage trends in connection with our Services. This allows us to understand, among other things, who is using the Services, how they are using them, where they get stuck, and other ways to improve the Services. These cookies and persistent device identifiers collect and store information including, but not limited to time of visit, pages visited, time spent on each page, IP address, unique device ID, advertising tags, and type of operating system used. We may retain aggregated logs or other anonymized versions of this data indefinitely.

Besides classic cookies, we may use similar techniques, such as flash cookies, tags, pixels, device fingerprinting, cross-device tracking, html5-local storage, javascripts, web beacons, software development kits (SDKs), etc. These techniques may be used to deliver cookies, track the number of visits to the Services, understand Service usage and effectiveness, and determine whether an email has been opened and acted upon.

### 2. Types of Cookies

Cookies can be categorized according to different criteria, one of which is the domain which placed the cookie. First-party cookies are those set by the website you are visiting (e.g. cookies placed by Company when you’re visiting the Website). Third-party cookies are cookies that are set by a domain other than the one you are visiting.

In addition, by categorizing cookies according to their purpose, you will receive more information on the benefits and the results of the cookie. In that context, we distinguish the following categories:

- **Strictly necessary cookies** are essential for a website to work properly;
- **Analytical / performance cookies** collect information about how you use the Website (e.g. which web pages/features you access most often, which allows us to improve the Website);
- **Functionality cookies** allow the Website to remember your choices (e.g. your username, language, currency, or region) and to provide enhanced, personalized, or customized features;
- **Targeting or advertising cookies** are used to deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you and your interests based on an analysis of your browsing.

As the first category of cookies are necessary for a website to function, these do not require your consent. Where the law requires your consent for the other categories of cookies, they can only be dropped with your consent, which you are free to give and withdraw anytime.

Your device may also offer you control over the use of cookies when you use our Services. You can find out further information on cookies and how to manage them on [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com) and [All About Cookies](https://www.allaboutcookies.org/). As these websites are not owned or operated by Company, we are not responsible for any of the content on them.

To opt out from and prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics across all websites, you’re welcome to check out the [Google Analytics opt-out instructions](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/181881?hl=en&ref_topic=2919631&sjid=2714987146502624768-EU).

You are free to refuse or delete cookies anytime, but this might affect the functionalities of the Services.

**4.1 Website Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.** When you first visit the Website, a cookie banner asks whether you accept analytical/performance and targeting or advertising cookies. Until you make a choice, we do not place these cookies or load the tags that set them. You can select “Accept all”, “Reject all”, or “Customize” to choose analytics and marketing separately. We remember your choice for 12 months and then ask again.

**4.2 Other Website Visitors.** Analytical/performance and targeting or advertising cookies are on by default. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, targeting or advertising cookies are off by default.

**4.3 Changing Your Choice.** You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page of the Website. If you turn a category off, we stop loading its tags and delete the cookies in that category that were placed on the trinsic.id domain. Cookies that third parties placed on their own domains must be deleted in your browser.

**4.4 Application.** The strictly necessary and functionality cookies described in Section 3.2 are needed to provide the parts of the Application you use. If you block them, those parts of the Application may not work.
